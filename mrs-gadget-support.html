Frequently Asked Questions

How do searches work?

Pick a photo (or take one), select the face, and Mrs. Gadget runs a visual search across the public web. You get ranked links to public pages where a visually similar face appears. Results are visual similarity rankings — they are not proof of identity.

Is my photo stored?

No. The face is detected and cropped on your device, metadata is stripped, and the crop is used only for the duration of the search. Photos are never stored on our servers, and no biometric templates are created. Search history lives only on your phone and can be deleted anytime in the app.

How do credits work?

Each search uses one credit. Subscriptions include a recurring credit allowance; credit packs never expire. A search that finds nothing does not cost a credit — it is returned automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Mrs. Gadget, and tap Cancel. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date prevents the next charge.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through them: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Search history and thumbnails are stored only on your device — deleting them in the app (History > Clear all) or deleting the app removes them. Server-side we keep only anonymous credit-ledger records required for purchase accounting. For any data request, email us.

Who may use this app?

Mrs. Gadget is for adults (18+). Search only your own face, or the face of an adult who gave you explicit permission. Do not upload photos of minors. Results must never be used for employment, housing, credit, insurance or any consequential decisions.
