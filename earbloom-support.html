I can't hear the examples

  • Turn the volume up — lesson sounds play even when the ringer switch is off.
  • If headphones were unplugged, playback stops on purpose; tap Play again.
  • After a phone call or another app's audio, tap Replay. A sound that fails to play is never counted as a wrong answer.

How lessons unlock

  • Each lesson explains one idea, then gives guided questions and ten questions on your own.
  • A lesson counts as practiced at 80% on your own. Guided answers and answers after a hint don't count toward the score.
  • Checkpoints close each unit; if one doesn't pass yet, review the lessons before it and try again.
  • Leaving a lesson keeps your place — tap Resume session to continue.

Too fast? Hard to tell apart?

  • Turn on Slower playback in Settings.
  • After a wrong answer, tap Compare to hear both options from the same starting note.
  • The Practice tab suggests the pair you confuse most.

Backup and new devices

  • Settings → Backup → Sign in with Apple backs up your progress; sign in with the same Apple ID on the new device.
  • Delete the backup from Settings → Backup → Delete account.

Purchases & restore

  • New device or reinstalled? Settings → Restore purchases (also on the paywall).
  • Cancel or change plan: Settings → Manage subscription (opens Apple's subscription settings).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
