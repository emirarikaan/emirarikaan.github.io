How a design is made

  1. Photograph the garden or outdoor space you want to change, or pick a photo from your library.
  2. Choose a transformation — a front yard or backyard makeover, a patio or poolside refresh, new walkways, a facade update, or your own written brief.
  3. Pick the space type and a style.
  4. Wait about half a minute. Turn on "notify me when it's done" and you can leave the app while it works.

Getting a better result

  • Shoot straight on from a doorway or corner, at eye level, with as much of the floor and ceiling in frame as you can.
  • Daylight beats a single ceiling bulb. Avoid heavy shadow and strong backlight.
  • One space per photo. A shot that mixes the whole street or several areas confuses the model.
  • Blurred or very dark photos produce blurred, unconvincing designs.

My result is blurred

Free designs are delivered as a blurred preview. The sharp, full-resolution image is unlocked by Garden Design Pro — the design you already generated is kept, so unlocking reveals that exact image rather than making you start again.

The before/after slider looks misaligned

Garden Design keeps the camera angle, boundaries, structures and layout of your photo. If the two halves do not line up, it is usually a very wide-angle shot or a photo taken at an extreme tilt — retake it more square to the space and try again.

Generation failed

A failed generation does not consume your free design. Check your connection and try again; if it keeps failing, email us with the approximate time and we will look at the logs.

Purchases & restore

  • New device or reinstalled? Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change plan: Settings → Manage Subscription (opens Apple's subscription sheet). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Languages

Garden Design is available in 24 languages and follows your device language on first launch. To change it afterwards: Settings → Language.

Deleting your designs

Deleting the app removes your anonymous identity and with it access to your stored designs. To have the files erased from the server too, email us and say so. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and for how long.
