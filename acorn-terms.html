1. What this service is

Acorn ("the App") is an iOS app that estimates the nutrition of your meals — calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber and sugar — from a photo or a text description using artificial intelligence, tracks what you eat against personalized daily targets, and shows your progress over time. Targets are calculated from the profile details you provide during setup (such as gender, age, height, weight, activity level and goal).
