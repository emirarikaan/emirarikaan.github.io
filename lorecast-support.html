Support — Weather Game

Need help? Email emirarikan99@gmail.com — include your iOS version and a screenshot if you can.

Location & "wrong city"

Weather Game uses location only while the app is open. If you denied access, the app shows a clearly labeled default or saved city instead — it never pretends that is your position. To fix: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Weather Game → While Using the App, or pick a city manually from the location pill at the top of the main screen.

Weather not updating

Forecasts refresh automatically, at most every ~15 minutes per location. Pull down on the main screen for a manual refresh. Offline? Weather Game shows the last real forecast with an "Updated X ago" tag.

Managing cities

Tap the city name (top left) → add up to 3 saved cities, swipe to delete, tap to switch.

Video scenes pause or don't play

In Low Power Mode and with Reduce Motion on, Weather Game intentionally shows still artwork to save battery and respect motion settings. Scenes also freeze on a frame after ~25 seconds by design; they resume when you return to the app.

Widgets

Widgets update on Apple's schedule, roughly hourly — exact minute-level freshness is not possible on iOS. If the widget shows a lock, open the app once while subscribed so it can refresh its access state.

Purchases & restore

  • New device or reinstalled? Settings → Restore Purchases (also available on the unlock screen).
  • Cancel or change plan: Settings → Manage Subscription (opens Apple's subscription sheet).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
