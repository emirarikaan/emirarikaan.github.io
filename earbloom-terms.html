1. Service

Earbloom teaches relative pitch — hearing intervals, scale degrees, chords, progressions and short melodies — through explanations, audio examples, guided and independent practice, and spaced review.

Learning, not diagnosis. Earbloom does not test hearing health, measure musical talent or give you perfect pitch. Scores describe your answers to the app's examples only. If you have concerns about your hearing, see a qualified professional. Keep the volume at a comfortable level.
