Privacy Policy — Earbloom

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Earbloom teaches ear training on your iPhone. Lessons, sounds and your progress work offline and stay on your device unless you choose to back them up. Earbloom never uses the microphone, shows no ads and doesn't sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

  • your answers to practice questions, lesson progress, review schedule and streak,
  • your onboarding choices (goal, experience, instrument) and your first "higher or lower" result,
  • settings such as slower playback, note names and reminder time.

All sounds are generated on your device from a bundled piano sound bank; nothing you hear is streamed.

Backup (optional)

If you turn on backup with Sign in with Apple (Settings → Backup), Earbloom creates an account with Google Firebase Authentication and stores your learning history in Google Cloud Firestore so you can restore it on another device: answers to practice questions, lesson progress and scores, and your onboarding choices. We don't request your name or email; Apple may give us a private relay identifier. Only you can read this data. You can delete it anytime in Settings → Backup → Delete account.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) to understand which lessons work and to fix crashes. These collect app-usage events (for example "lesson practiced" with the lesson number and a score such as 8 of 10), device model, iOS version and an app-instance identifier. They never include audio, your name, email or anything you type. This data is not used for advertising or tracking. Firebase Remote Config is used to switch features on or off.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, processes an app-generated identifier (or your backup account identifier, if you use backup) and purchase receipt information to validate access. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

No tracking, no ads

Earbloom contains no advertising and performs no cross-app tracking. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you.

Permissions

  • Notifications — only if you turn on the daily reminder; reminders are scheduled on your device.

Earbloom does not ask for microphone, location, contacts or photo access.

Your control

  • Reset all progress on this device: Settings → Reset progress on this device. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Delete your backup account and backed-up progress: Settings → Backup → Delete account.
  • Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Earbloom is a general-audience learning app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this page with a new date.
