Logging a period or a day

On the Today tab, tap Log today to record flow, pain, symptoms, moods and notes. Tap any day in the Cycle calendar to log or edit a past day.

Predictions look off

Estimates start from the cycle and period length you set during onboarding (More → Cycle setup) and get more personal as you log more cycles. Irregular cycles naturally make estimates less certain.

Reminders

Turn reminders on in More → Reminders. If they don't appear, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → CycleCare.

Your data

  • Your logs live on this iPhone. Export them from More → Privacy & data → Export data.
  • Delete everything from More → Privacy & data → Delete all data.
  • Deleting the app removes its data — export first if you want to keep a copy.

Purchases & restore

  • New device or reinstalled? Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in More.
  • Cancel or change plan: More → Manage subscription (opens Apple's subscription settings).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
