Terms of Use — Little Me: Inner Child Journal

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. What this service is

Little Me: Inner Child Journal ("the App") is a private self-reflection journal for adults. You write about what you're feeling, and — when you ask for it — the App generates supportive AI reflections, gentler rewordings of harsh self-talk, and weekly summaries using Google's Gemini models. The App also includes small self-care tools (breathing, grounding, kind words) and an optional illustrated "Little Me" avatar.

The App is not therapy, counseling, a medical or mental-health service, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency support, and it does not replace professional care. AI responses are supportive self-reflection text, not professional advice. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
