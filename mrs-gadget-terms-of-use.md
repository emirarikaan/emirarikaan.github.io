# Terms of Use — Mrs. Gadget: Face Search AI

_Last updated: August 9, 2026_

## 1. What this service is
Mrs. Gadget: Face Search AI is a face-focused, public-web visual search utility for adults. You submit a photo, the app crops the selected face on device, and our backend runs a visual search through third-party providers (currently SerpApi's Google Lens API), returning links to public web pages. It is **not** a biometric identity-verification service, background-check product, or people-search directory.

## 2. Eligibility — 18+
You must be at least 18 years old (or older where local law requires). Do not upload photos of minors.

## 3. Permission and acceptable use
Search only your own face, or that of an adult who gave explicit permission or for whom you have a lawful right. Prohibited: harassment, stalking, surveillance, blackmail, doxxing; photos of minors; credit/employment/housing/tenant/insurance or similar eligibility decisions; judging a person by the website a result came from; any unlawful use.

## 4. Credits and subscriptions
- One completed search that returns results consumes one credit.
- A failed search, or one that finds no matches, does not consume a credit — it is returned automatically.
- Subscription credits reset each billing period and do not roll over; purchased packs do not expire.
- Free preview searches may be offered; unlocking a preview's results consumes one credit.
- Payments and refunds are handled by Apple; subscriptions renew unless cancelled in App Store settings.

## 5. No accuracy guarantee
Results can be incomplete or wrong; similar-looking people cause false positives. Ranking is visual-search order, not identity probability. Provider-supplied similarity scores are image-similarity estimates, not identity proof.

SerpApi and Google are independent; neither endorses the app. Result categories are our own classification.
