Privacy Policy — PerfectBrows

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · listed on the App Store as "Brow Design: AI Eyebrow Shape"

PerfectBrows needs one thing from you: a photo of your face. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Because that photo is a selfie and not a picture of a room, this page is explicit about what happens to it — including the parts that are less than obvious.

No account, no profile

PerfectBrows asks for no sign-up, no email address and no name. The app signs in anonymously to Firebase, which issues a random identifier so your analyses can be returned to your device. That identifier is not linked to you, and we hold no name, email, phone number or address.

Your selfie — what actually happens

When you ask for an analysis, the photo you took or chose is uploaded to our Firebase Storage bucket (Google Cloud, United States) and then:

  • sent to Google's Gemini API, which reads your face shape and brow shape and writes the assessment, the harmony score and the styling steps you read under the result;
  • sent to fal.ai, which renders the visualization of your face with the brow style you picked;
  • stored alongside the result so the before/after comparison still works when you reopen the analysis.

Your original photo is deleted automatically 30 days after upload by a scheduled job. The generated result stays in your library until you delete the app or ask us to remove it.

We do not run face recognition and we do not build a face template, a faceprint or any biometric identifier. The model reads shape and proportion to produce a cosmetic suggestion, and nothing about your face is used to identify you or matched against any other photo.

How stored images are served — please read this one

Images are delivered to the app over public storage.googleapis.com links. The address of each file contains your random identifier and a random analysis identifier, so it cannot be guessed or browsed, and it is not listed anywhere. But it is not password-protected: anyone who obtains the exact link can open the image, and the link does not expire.

In practice the link is only ever sent to your own device. Still, these are photos of your face, so treat a PerfectBrows image link the way you would treat any unlisted link to a personal photo — if you share it, whoever receives it can keep opening it. If you would rather your selfies were not handled this way, please do not upload them.

What else we store

  • Your analyses — the face shape and brow style the model identified, the score, the written assessment, the style you picked and the image paths, kept under your anonymous identifier.
  • Your quiz answers — the six onboarding answers about your brow shape, density, routine, struggle, current method and goal. They are sent with each analysis so the result is tailored, and they say nothing about who you are.
  • Usage counters — how many analyses you have generated, so the free allowance and subscription limits can be enforced.
  • Feature requests and feedback — only if you submit them. Anything you type there is visible to other users on the public request board, so do not include personal details.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through your Apple Account. RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and receipt information to confirm whether your subscription is active. PerfectBrows never sees your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Diagnostics

PerfectBrows uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to count anonymous events (which screens are opened, whether an analysis succeeded) and to receive crash reports. These carry no photo content and no personal identifiers. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Notifications

If you turn on "notify me when it's done", the app schedules a local notification on your device. No push server is involved and no notification data leaves your phone.

No tracking, no ads

PerfectBrows shows no advertising and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because we do not track.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your anonymous identity from your device, which means the analyses stored under it can no longer be reached from the app. To have the stored photos and records erased from the server as well, email eacompany947@gmail.com from the device in question and say so; we will delete them.

Children

PerfectBrows is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
