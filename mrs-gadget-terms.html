Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. What this service is

Mrs. Gadget: Face Search AI ("the App") is a face-focused, public-web visual search utility for adults. You submit a photo, the App detects and crops the selected face on your device, and our backend runs a visual search through third-party providers (currently SerpApi's Google Lens API). The App returns links to publicly available web pages where visually similar images appear.

The App is not a biometric identity-verification service, not a background-check product, and not a people-search directory. Results are visual-search rankings of public pages, never a confirmation of anyone's identity.
