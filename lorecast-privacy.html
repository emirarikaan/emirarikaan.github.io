Privacy Policy — Weather Game

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Weather Game is built to know as little about you as possible. No account, no ads, no tracking — your weather stays between you and Apple's weather service.

No account, no profile

Weather Game requires no sign-up, login, or profile. We operate no server that stores your personal data.

Location

With your permission, your device location is used only while the app is in use, and only to request your local forecast from Apple's weather service (Apple Weather / WeatherKit) on your device. Coordinates are transmitted to Apple to return the forecast, subject to Apple's privacy terms. Weather Game:

  • does not upload your location to any server of its own,
  • does not track location in the background,
  • does not build a history of the places you have been.

You can decline location access entirely and search for a city manually. Saved cities are stored only on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, processes an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipt information to validate access. Weather Game never sees your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

No tracking, no ads

Weather Game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, and performs no cross-app tracking. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track.

Data stored on your device

Preferences (world theme, temperature unit, saved cities) and a cached copy of the latest forecast are stored locally, and in the app's own App Group container so the Home Screen widget can read them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Weather Game is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app and is posted at this page with a new date.
