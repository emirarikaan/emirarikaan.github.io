# Privacy Policy - Mrs. Gadget

Last updated: August 9, 2026

## Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how Mrs. Gadget ("the App") handles information when you use its mobile product discovery features. The App is provided by Emir Arikan ("Service Provider", "we", "us").

Mrs. Gadget helps users identify gadgets, accessories, electronics, and similar products from images. It may compare a submitted photo against public web results or third-party product search systems and return possible matches, product pages, thumbnails, and related information.

## 1. Information We Process

### 1.1 Images and Search Inputs
When you choose to run a search, the App may process:

- Photos selected from your library or captured with your camera
- Cropped or adjusted versions of submitted images
- Text prompts, labels, categories, or filters you enter for a search
- Search result data such as thumbnails, product titles, source links, similarity signals, prices, store names, and listing metadata

### 1.2 App and Device Data
To operate and improve the App, we may collect limited technical information, including:

- Device type, operating system, language, app version, and region settings
- Anonymous identifiers such as Firebase installation identifiers, app instance identifiers, IDFV, RevenueCat identifiers, and similar technical IDs
- Usage events, feature interactions, diagnostics, performance data, and crash reports
- Local settings such as onboarding progress, search preferences, credits, sound, or haptic choices

### 1.3 Purchases and Support
If you buy credits or subscribe, the App may receive purchase status, entitlement information, transaction identifiers, and renewal status from Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full card number or payment card details.

If you contact support, we may process your email address and the content of your message so we can respond.

## 2. How We Use Information
Information is used for the following purposes:

- Running visual product searches requested by you
- Showing likely gadget matches, links, images, and product information
- Saving local history and preferences on your device
- Managing credits, subscriptions, trials, purchases, and restore-purchase flows
- Protecting the backend from abuse, automated misuse, and fraudulent activity
- Understanding general app performance and fixing bugs or crashes
- Providing customer support and responding to legal requests when required

## 3. Product Photos
Photos are processed to provide the search you request. Mrs. Gadget does not sell submitted photos and does not use them for advertising.

Submitted images may be sent to backend systems or third-party visual search, AI, marketplace, or image matching providers. These providers may process the image and return possible product matches, source pages, images, prices, or related metadata.

Some preparation, such as cropping, framing, or image quality checks, may happen on your device before a search is sent.

## 4. Local History
The App may keep search history on your device. This can include a submitted image, product thumbnails, source links, search terms, prices, and similar result information. You can clear local history inside the App where available, or remove it by deleting the App from your device.

## 5. Service Providers
Mrs. Gadget may rely on third-party services to provide core functionality and app operations, including:

- Visual search, AI, product lookup, marketplace, or image matching providers
- Firebase Authentication for anonymous identity and session support
- Firebase App Check for request validation and backend protection
- Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config for analytics, diagnostics, crash reporting, and configuration
- Apple App Store for distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and refund handling
- RevenueCat for purchase, entitlement, credit, and subscription management

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and service terms.

## 6. Sharing
We share information only as needed to operate the App, perform requested searches, manage purchases, secure the service, analyze reliability, respond to support requests, or comply with legal obligations.

Submitted photos are shared with visual search or product matching providers only to complete the search requested by you. We do not sell personal information.

## 7. Permissions
The App may ask for:

- **Camera**, so you can photograph a gadget or product
- **Photo Library**, so you can choose an existing image
- **Notifications**, if reminder or result notification features are offered

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## 8. Children
The App is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old to use Mrs. Gadget, or older where local law requires a higher minimum age.

## 9. Prohibited Content
Please do not submit images containing payment cards, passwords, private documents, addresses, personal identification numbers, confidential workplace materials, or other sensitive information unless you have the right and legal basis to do so.

## 10. Retention

- Local history remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App.
- Submitted images are processed for the requested search and are not intentionally kept by Mrs. Gadget as a permanent product database unless a feature clearly says otherwise.
- Technical identifiers, purchase records, diagnostics, analytics, and security logs may be retained as needed for operations, billing, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, and legal compliance.
- Third-party providers may retain certain logs or search-related data under their own policies.

## 11. Your Choices
You can clear local App data where controls are available, revoke camera or photo access in iOS Settings, cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings, or uninstall the App.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. To make a request, contact eacompany947@gmail.com.

## 12. Security
We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted transmission where supported. However, no mobile app, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 13. Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
