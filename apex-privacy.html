Privacy Policy — Apex

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Apex records your drives on your iPhone. Your routes, locations, trip history, notes and photos stay on your device. We don't sell or share personal data, and we never upload your routes.

Location

Apex uses your location to record drives you start (manual mode, "While Using" permission) or that it detects (automatic mode, "Always" permission, only if you turn it on). Recorded samples are stored in the app's protected storage on your iPhone and are used to show your trips, maps, statistics, share cards, replay videos and widgets. Motion data (if you allow it) only helps tell driving apart from walking, trains and flights; it isn't stored.

What stays on your device

  • trip routes, times, distances, speeds and driving-style estimates,
  • your vehicles, notes, tags and trip photos,
  • privacy zones (for example Home and Work).

Share cards, videos and exports are created on your device and only go where you send them. Their route start and end are trimmed and privacy zones are hidden.

Clubs (optional)

Clubs are the only feature that uses a server (Google Firebase). If you create or join a club, Apex creates a private account identifier for you (anonymous, or linked to Sign in with Apple if you choose — we don't request your name or email). Club members can see:

  • the display name you choose,
  • this week's totals from verified drives: distance, driving time, number of drives, days on the road and scenic-route count.

Your routes, locations and trip details are never uploaded to clubs. During an optional group drive you explicitly join, your live position is shared with that club until you stop sharing, go "ghost", or the session ends; live positions are deleted when you leave and expire automatically on the server within hours.

You can leave clubs anytime, and delete your account and club data in Settings → Account → Delete account. Reports you send about a member are stored so we can review them.

Analytics and crash reports

With your permission (Settings → Help improve Apex), we use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. These collect app-usage events (for example "drive saved" with a distance range such as "5–20 km"), device model, iOS version and an app-instance identifier. They never include locations, routes, place names, trip titles, notes or photos. Firebase Remote Config is used to switch features on or off. You can turn analytics and crash reports off anytime.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, processes an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipt information to validate access. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

No tracking, no ads

Apex contains no advertising and performs no cross-app tracking. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you.

Permissions

  • Location — to record drives (and group drives you join).
  • Motion & Fitness — optional, to tell driving apart from other movement.
  • Photos — only to add photos you pick to a trip and to save cards and videos you create.
  • Notifications — local alerts such as "Drive saved" or "Was this a drive?"; generated on your device.

Your control

  • Edit, split, merge, reclassify or delete any trip.
  • Export every trip as GPX plus a CSV summary from Settings → Your data.
  • Delete all trips from Settings → Your data → Delete all trips. Deleting the app also removes them.
  • Delete your club account and club data from Settings → Account → Delete account.

Safety

Don't interact with your phone while driving. Apex is designed to record hands-free.

Children

Apex is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.
