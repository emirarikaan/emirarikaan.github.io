Privacy Policy — House Design

Last updated: August 30, 2026

House Design needs one thing from you: a photo of a room. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. This page explains exactly what happens to that photo, including the parts that are less than obvious.

No account, no profile

House Design asks for no sign-up, no email address and no name. The app signs in anonymously to Firebase, which issues a random identifier so your designs can be returned to your device. That identifier is not linked to you, and we hold no name, email, phone number or address.

Your photos — what actually happens

When you ask for a design, the photo you chose is uploaded to our Firebase Storage bucket (Google Cloud, United States) and then:

  • sent to Google's Gemini API, which describes the room and writes the change list you read under the result;
  • sent to fal.ai, which renders the redesigned image;
  • stored alongside the result so the before/after slider still works when you reopen the design.

Your original photo is deleted automatically 30 days after upload by a scheduled job. The generated result stays in your library until you delete the app or ask us to remove it.

How stored images are served — please read this one

Images are delivered to the app over public storage.googleapis.com links. The address of each file contains your random identifier and a random design identifier, so it cannot be guessed or browsed, and it is not listed anywhere. But it is not password-protected: anyone who obtains the exact link can open the image, and the link does not expire.

In practice the link is only ever sent to your own device. Still, treat a House Design image link the way you would treat any unlisted link — if you share it, whoever receives it can keep opening it. If you would rather your room photos were not handled this way, please do not upload them.

What else we store

  • Your designs — the title, the change list, the room and style you picked, and the image paths, kept under your anonymous identifier.
  • Usage counters — how many designs you have generated, so the free allowance and subscription limits can be enforced.
  • Feature requests and feedback — only if you submit them. Anything you type there is visible to other users on the public request board, so do not include personal details.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through your Apple Account. RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and receipt information to confirm whether your subscription is active. House Design never sees your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Diagnostics

House Design uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to count anonymous events (which screens are opened, whether a generation succeeded) and to receive crash reports. These carry no photo content and no personal identifiers. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Notifications

If you turn on "notify me when it's done", the app schedules a local notification on your device. No push server is involved and no notification data leaves your phone.

No tracking, no ads

House Design shows no advertising and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because we do not track.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your anonymous identity from your device, which means the designs stored under it can no longer be reached from the app. To have the stored files and records erased from the server as well, email emirarikan99@gmail.com from the device in question and say so; we will delete them.

Children

House Design is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this page with a new date.
